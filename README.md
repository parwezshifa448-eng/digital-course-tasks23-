### 1. Module 1: Lead Fundamentals & Digital Marketing Funnel Architecture
* **What is a Lead?** Digital marketing aur Facebook Ads ki duniya mein Lead ka matlab hota hai kisi prospective customer ya student ki Contact Information (jaise Name, Email Address, Phone Number, City, ya Course Interest)[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Jab koi unknown user ad dekh kar apni contact details deta hai, toh woh ek Lead ban jata hai[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
* **Cold Audience vs. Lead vs. Customer (3 Stages):**
  * **Top of Funnel (TOFU):** Cold Audience / Strangers - Aapke business ko bilkul nahi jaante. Action: Brand Awareness & Free Offer[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
  * **Middle of Funnel (MOFU):** Prospect / Lead - Interest dikhaya aur Phone/Email share kiya. Action: Trust Building & Value Nurturing[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
  * **Bottom of Funnel (BOFU):** Hot Customer / Admission - Course fee pay karke admission le liya. Action: Course Delivery & Satisfaction[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
* **NITZ Real-World Practical Example:** Nalanda It Zone (NITZ) Patna campus ke baahar se guzarne wala har student Cold Audience hai[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). Jab woh enquiry counter par aakar apna Naam aur WhatsApp Number register karwata hai, toh woh Lead ban jata hai[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). Jab woh Tally ya Web Dev course ki fee submit karta hai, tab woh Enrolled Student / Customer banta hai[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).

### 2. Module 2: What is a Lead Magnet & The Value Exchange Engine
* **What is a Lead Magnet?** Lead Magnet ek aisa free, high-value incentive ya gift hota hai jo businesses prospective customers ko offer karte hain unki personal contact details (Name, WhatsApp Number, Email) lene ke liye[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). Jaise magnet lohe ko apni taraf khinchta hai, waise hi Lead Magnet prospective students aur buyers ko aapke ad par click karke details submit karne ke liye attract karta hai[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
* **Why Strangers Don't Give Contact Details For Free:** Aaj ke time mein koi bhi stranger direct aapke kehne par apna WhatsApp Number ya Email share nahi karega kyunki unhe Spam calls ka darr rehta hai[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span). Jab aap unhe kuch Free & Valuable offer karte hain (jaise Free E-Book, Free Demo Class), tab woh khushi-khushi apni details share karte hain, ise Two-Way Value Exchange kehte hain[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* **NITZ Practical Lead Magnet Strategy:** Agar NITZ direct ad chalaye 'Join Web Dev Course for Rs. 5000', toh response kam aayega[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span). Lekin agar NITZ ad chalaye 'Download Free Web Development Roadmap PDF & HTML Cheat Sheet', toh hazaaron students instant form fill karke PDF download karenge, aur NITZ ko 500+ verified student leads mil jayengi[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).

### 6. Module 6: Master Summary, Beginner Bugs & NITZ Practical Lab
* **Top 3 Student Beginner Bugs & Debugging Fixes:**
  1. *Weak or Generic Lead Magnet Bug:* 'Subscribe to Newsletter' offer karne par zero leads milengi. Fix: Specific offer dein 'Download 6-Day Python Cheatsheet PDF'.
  2. *High Form Dropouts (Too Many Fields):* 10 fields (Address, Qualification, DOB) puchne par users form chor dete hain. Fix: Sirf Name, WhatsApp Number aur Email maangein.
  3. *Slow Follow-Up Failure:* 2 din baad call karne par lead dead ho jata hai. Fix: Instant WhatsApp auto-responder setup karein.
